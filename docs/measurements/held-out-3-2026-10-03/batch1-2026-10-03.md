## A gap in R6, found after the draw, recorded and NOT repaired in this batch

R6 accepts the first fix commit an advisory cites if that commit touches 1 to 10 non-test JS/TS
files. It does not check that those files hold the defect. Two admitted cases show the gap:
- **Case 05 (icehrm):** the advisory's own references point to
  `core/src/Employees/Rest/EmployeeSkillsRestEndPoint.php`, a PHP file. The cited commit's only
  non-test JS/TS file, `core/execute/execute.js`, is one it deletes, and nothing in the advisory
  connects it to the defect. The defect sits in PHP, outside every Fixor lane.
- **Case 06 (clawhub):** the advisory describes a missing authorization check in the changelog
  preview. The commit it cites is titled "fix(security): authenticate anonymous API quota
  identities", and its files are rate-limit and client-IP code. The cited commit does not appear
  to be the fix for this advisory.

**Both cases stay admitted.** Dropping them now would be a choice made after looking, which is
what the frozen rule exists to prevent. Read against the advisories, 4 of the 6 have defect files
consistent with their advisory: cases 01, 02, 03 and 04. **Every measurement pre-registration on
this set must state, before it runs, how it treats cases 05 and 06.** The recommended treatment
is to report every admitted case and, separately, the four with consistent defect files.

Closing the gap changes the rule, and under the admission file a different rule is a different
set. So later batches drawn under these rules keep the gap. A corrected rule is a new set, named
and pre-registered before it is drawn.

## What the counts say about the design

- **The binding constraint is fix citation, not CWE class or language.** 126 advisories in
  application-shaped repositories, in a lane, cite no same-repository commit or pull request.
  The rule as written cannot reach them.
- **R7 did its job.** Seven advisories published after June had fixes dated in May or June 2026,
  public before the cutoff.
- **Rate.** Three months of advisories gave 6 admitted cases, 4 of them consistent with their
  advisory. The monthly re-draw would bring the set to 10 around early 2027, and to 30 well into
  2028.
