## Pricing & billing

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is the free tier really free?">
    Yes — 5 scans / month on public repos with all four detectors,
    no card. The cap exists because every scan calls Anthropic and
    that has a real cost; we'd rather pause than overspend on
    someone who hasn't subscribed.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What does my card statement say?">
    Paddle, not Fixor. Paddle is our merchant of record — they
    handle VAT, sales tax, and chargebacks for the regions we
    serve.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I cancel any time?">
    Yes — open
    <a href="https://app.fixor.dev">app.fixor.dev</a>, navigate to
    `/orgs/<id>/billing`, and click `Cancel`. The cancellation
    takes effect at the end of the current paid period; you keep
    the tier until then. We don't pro-rate.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What's the refund policy?">
    14 days, defect-only. Email
    <a href="mailto:support@fixor.dev">support@fixor.dev</a> if
    Fixor was unusable for you due to something on our side.
    EU-statutory rights apply on top.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Privacy & data

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do you store my code?">
    No. The PR diff is processed in memory and discarded. We store
    metadata (which scans ran, what they cost, what severity
    findings landed) but the diff content itself is never written
    to our database. The
    [Privacy Policy](https://fixor.dev/privacy.html) has the full
    retention table.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Anthropic train on my code?">
    No. Anthropic's API terms exclude commercial customer data
    from training. We don't opt into any "improve our model"
    setting. See
    [Anthropic's trust center](https://trust.anthropic.com/) for
    their commitments.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I get a list of who has access to my org's data?">
    Email
    <a href="mailto:support@fixor.dev">support@fixor.dev</a> for
    a full data export of every row that references your org —
    same shape as the GDPR access right. We respond within 30
    days.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens when I uninstall?">
    GitHub revokes our access immediately. The org row in our DB
    is deleted after a 30-day grace window (so an accidental
    uninstall doesn't lose your settings). Reports purge on the
    same schedule, capped at 90 days.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Accuracy

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What's the false-positive rate?">
    Lower than regex-based scanners because Claude reads the
    surrounding code. We don't publish a single number because it
    varies hugely by codebase shape. The
    [no-warranty section](https://fixor.dev/terms.html) of our
    terms is honest about this — every finding is a suggestion,
    not a guarantee.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Will Fixor catch every vulnerability?">
    No security tool will. Fixor is a signal in a layered defense
    — code review + pen testing + a SAST/SCA scanner sit
    alongside it. Use it where its detector families fit and
    don't pretend it covers what it doesn't (yet).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A finding is wrong for my code. What do I do?">
    Two options in the dashboard's `/orgs/<id>/settings`:
    raise the **severity threshold** (drops low-severity noise
    globally), or add the file's path to **ignored globs**
    (skips a specific path). Both apply on the next scan. There's
    no learning loop — Fixor doesn't &ldquo;remember&rdquo; your
    feedback per finding (today).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I disable a specific detector?">
    Yes. The settings page has a detector-allowlist mode: pick
    which of the four families you want to run. Useful if your
    codebase doesn't render HTML server-side and you don't want
    XSS noise.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Operations

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What does Fixor need permission to do on GitHub?">
    `pull_requests: read & write`, `contents: read-only`,
    `metadata: read-only`. We post the comment + read the diff and
    nothing else. The
    [marketplace listing](https://github.com/marketplace/fixor)
    has the per-permission justification table.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where does the analysis run?">
    Backend on Railway (the webhook handler + scan workflow), the
    Anthropic call goes to their US edge, the dashboard runs on
    Vercel, and the database is Neon (US or EU depending on your
    Neon project). Full subprocessor list:
    <a href="https://fixor.dev/security.html">fixor.dev/security</a>.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is Fixor open source?">
    Yes — MIT-licensed at
    <a href="https://github.com/tornidomaroc-web/fixor">github.com/tornidomaroc-web/fixor</a>.
    The hosted service runs the same code; you can verify any
    claim in this docs site against the source.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I report a security issue in Fixor itself?">
    Email <a href="mailto:support@fixor.dev">support@fixor.dev</a>
    with subject `SECURITY:`. Full scope, in/out of scope, and
    safe-harbor terms are at
    <a href="https://fixor.dev/security.html">fixor.dev/security</a>.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
